Custom rule

Normally leave this empty: the zone you pick already carries its daylight-saving rule. Fill it in only for a place the list gets wrong, or to test. When filled in, it replaces the zone's rule.

It is a POSIX timezone rule, for example EST5EDT,M3.2.0,M11.1.0 (New York):

  • EST — name of standard time
  • 5 — hours behind UTC; the sign is reversed, so -5:30 means 5½ hours ahead
  • EDT — name of daylight time, one hour ahead of standard time unless an offset follows it
  • M3.2.0 — daylight time starts in month 3, week 2, on day 0 (Sunday): the second Sunday of March
  • M11.1.0 — it ends on the first Sunday of November
  • Week 5 means the last such day of the month. A change happens at 02:00 unless a time follows, e.g. M3.5.0/1 for 01:00.

A place without daylight saving is just a name and an offset, such as IST-5:30. Names that are not letters go in angle brackets, such as <+0530>-5:30.
